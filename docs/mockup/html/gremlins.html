Gremlins Simple scripted pests, not AI. One drawing each, used on the picker, the timeline markers and in the scenes. Pumpkin orange = gremlin.
Masher Presses a button over and over, fast.
Bouncer Makes a button or sensor flicker for a split second, like a loose wire.
Stuck Holds a button down and never lets go.
Dropout Makes a signal that is normally on cut out for a moment.
Spike Makes a dial-type reading (like a temperature sensor) jump suddenly.
Drifter Slowly pushes a dial-type reading up or down.
Dashed tiles: these need inputs the bundled examples don't have; they hide behind "3 more need other inputs".
