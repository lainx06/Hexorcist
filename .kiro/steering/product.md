# Product
Hexorcist checks small ATmega328P programs before they go on a board. Users pick plain-English rules; seeded
gremlins attack the inputs on an emulated chip in the browser; a failure becomes a shortened, replayable trace.
Pitch: "Before you flash AI-written firmware, let the gremlins try it first."
Audience: students, teachers, makers, and anyone flashing AI-written firmware.
Voice: plain, friendly, blunt. Never promise. Never use the words safe, secure, guaranteed, certified, real-time,
first, unique, or shortest in UI copy, docs, or video scripts.
